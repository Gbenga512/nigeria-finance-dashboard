# MScFE Research Framework

## Working research direction

**Development and Evaluation of a Quantitative Financial Intelligence System for Market Risk, Liquidity Risk and Decision Support in Emerging Markets**

## Research architecture

Market data → data validation → return/feature engineering → descriptive statistics → volatility and tail-risk models → portfolio risk attribution → stress testing → out-of-sample validation → management decision support.

## Empirical discipline

The platform must distinguish clearly between observed data, model estimates and management interpretation. Nigeria-specific conclusions should only be made when supported by traceable Nigerian datasets. Missing observations must be reported rather than fabricated.

## Current implemented research modules

- Cross-asset comparative statistics
- Rolling annualized volatility
- Rolling Sharpe ratio
- Historical multi-horizon stress scenarios
- Portfolio VaR and Expected Shortfall
- Monte Carlo VaR and Expected Shortfall
- Correlation and component risk
- VaR backtesting

## Planned research extensions

1. GARCH-family conditional volatility models.
2. Regime detection and clustering.
3. Machine-learning classification of risk regimes.
4. Walk-forward model evaluation.
5. Kupiec and Christoffersen VaR backtests.
6. Nigeria-specific macro/market factors from traceable public datasets.
7. Reproducible experiment registry and downloadable research tables.
